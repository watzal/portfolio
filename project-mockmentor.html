01

Overview

Most interview prep tools ask generic questions.
MockMentor is different, it reads your actual resume and the job profile you are targeting, then generates questions that are directly relevant to your background and the role.
It knows your work experience, your projects, your skills, EVERYTHING!
It asks accordingly.

Built with LangChain orchestration, Google Gemini as the LLM backbone, and Streamlit for a clean deployable UI. MLflow handles experiment tracking so you can see how your answers improve over sessions.

02

The Problem

Generic interview prep is broken.
Platforms give you the same questions regardless of who you are or what role you are targeting. A data scientist with an ML background and an analyst switching careers should not get the same questions — but they do.

MockMentor solves this with a simple insight: the best interview prep is contextual. Feed it your resume and the JD, and every question, hint, and piece of feedback is personalised to the intersection of your experience and the role’s requirements.

04

Technical Architecture

Three-stage LangChain pipeline: ingestion → generation → evaluation.
Each stage is a composable chain, making it easy to swap models or add new question types without touching the rest of the system.

05

Challenges & Learnings

PDF resume parsing is messier than it looks. Different resume formats, fonts, and layouts produce wildly different text extraction results. Building a normalisation layer that cleans and structures the extracted text before feeding it to the LLM was essential for consistent question quality.
LangChain’s composable chain architecture made the evaluation pipeline surprisingly clean. Each stage: parse, generate, evaluate, feedback is independently testable and swappable, which proved valuable when switching models mid-build without breaking anything.
